KLOKSIE — CUSTOMER POLICIES

Terms &
Conditions.

These policies govern your use of the Kloksie website, online store, products, services, and related platforms. By accessing our website or placing an order, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the website or place an order.

1. About Kloksie

Kloksie is an online fashion store offering curated clothing, footwear, accessories, and other fashion-related products.

Kloksie may update its products, prices, availability, website content, policies, and services from time to time.

2. Eligibility

By placing an order with Kloksie, you confirm that:

  • The information you provide is accurate and complete.
  • You are legally capable of entering into a purchase transaction.
  • If you are under the age of majority, you are using the website with the involvement or permission of a parent or legal guardian.
  • You will use the website only for lawful purposes.

3. Products

We make reasonable efforts to ensure that product descriptions, photographs, measurements, colors, and other information are accurate.

  • Actual colors may vary depending on your device or screen settings.
  • Minor measurement differences may occur.
  • Product availability may change without prior notice.
  • Some products may have limited quantities.
  • Product photographs may contain styling elements that are not included with the purchase.

For questions about measurements, condition, material, authenticity, or other product details, customers are encouraged to contact Kloksie before placing an order.

4. Product Condition

Products sold by Kloksie may include new, or intentionally aged pieces. Where applicable, the product listing will indicate its relevant condition.

This does not exclude customers' rights regarding products that are defective, materially different from the description, or otherwise non-conforming.

5. Prices

All prices displayed on the website are in Philippine Pesos (PHP), unless otherwise stated.

Kloksie reserves the right to change prices at any time. A price change will not affect an order that has already been successfully confirmed, except where correction of an obvious pricing or listing error is necessary.

6. Ordering

  1. Select your desired product.
  2. Select any required variation, such as size or color.
  3. Add the product to your cart.
  4. Review your order details.
  5. Provide accurate customer and delivery information.
  6. Select an available payment method.
  7. Complete payment where required.
  8. Wait for your order confirmation.

Customers are responsible for reviewing their order before completing checkout.

7. Order Confirmation

Submitting an order does not automatically guarantee acceptance.

Kloksie may cancel or decline an order if the item is no longer available, there is an obvious pricing or listing error, the customer provided materially incorrect information, a payment cannot be verified, there is reasonable evidence of fraudulent or unauthorized activity, or circumstances beyond Kloksie's reasonable control prevent fulfillment.

If Kloksie cancels an order after payment has been successfully received, any applicable refund will be processed according to this Refund Policy and applicable law.

8. Product Availability

Because some Kloksie products may be limited or one-of-a-kind pieces, availability may change quickly.

If an item becomes unavailable after an order has been placed, Kloksie will contact the customer regarding available options, which may include a replacement, store credit where appropriate, or refund.

9. Product Variations

Where a product has selectable variations such as size, color, quantity, or other available options, customers must select the correct variation before completing checkout.

Kloksie is not responsible for a customer selecting an incorrect variation unless the item delivered differs from the confirmed order.

10. Payment

Kloksie accepts payment methods displayed during checkout.

Payment must be successfully completed before an order is processed, unless Kloksie expressly provides another payment arrangement.

Kloksie does not intentionally store complete payment card information when payment is processed through a third-party payment provider. Payment transactions may be handled by third-party payment processors subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Shipping

Kloksie ships orders to the delivery address provided by the customer.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that their full name, mobile number, complete address, barangay, city/municipality, province, postal code, and other required delivery information are accurate.

Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to courier operations, weather, holidays, high-volume periods, customs procedures, remote-area delivery, or circumstances beyond Kloksie's control. Once an order has been handed to the courier, delivery is also subject to the courier's operations.

12. Failed Delivery

Customers should make reasonable efforts to receive their orders.

If a parcel is returned to Kloksie because of an incorrect or incomplete address, recipient unavailability, refusal to receive the parcel, failure to coordinate with the courier, repeated failed delivery attempts, or other circumstances attributable to the customer, additional shipping costs may apply before the parcel can be resent.

Where applicable, any refund may be reduced by non-refundable shipping or other costs actually incurred by Kloksie, subject to applicable law.

13. Shipping Delays

Kloksie is not responsible for delays caused by events outside our reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disasters, courier disruptions, holidays, transport interruptions, government restrictions, public emergencies, incorrect customer information, or other circumstances beyond Kloksie's reasonable control.

Customers may contact Kloksie for assistance with tracking and delivery concerns.

14. Pre-Order Products

Products identified as Pre-Order are not immediately available for dispatch.

Estimated release, arrival, or shipping dates are provided for guidance and may change. Pre-order customers acknowledge that fulfillment may take longer than regular in-stock orders.

If a significant delay occurs, Kloksie will provide an appropriate update and available options.

15. Cancellation Policy

Customers may request cancellation before an order has been processed or shipped.

Cancellation requests should be sent through Instagram: @kloksie.co.

Once an order has already been processed, packed, or shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

Where cancellation is accepted, any applicable refund will be processed according to the Refund Policy and applicable law.

16. Return & Exchange Policy

Kloksie aims to provide accurate product descriptions, measurements, photographs, and information before purchase.

For ordinary change-of-mind requests, such as ordering the wrong size, choosing the wrong color, no longer wanting the item, or changing your mind after purchase, returns or exchanges are generally not guaranteed unless Kloksie expressly approves the request.

However, this policy does not remove or limit any rights that customers may have under applicable Philippine consumer protection laws.

17. Wrong, Damaged, or Defective Items

If you receive an item that is different from what you ordered, arrives damaged, has a material defect, is missing a component, or otherwise does not conform to the order, please contact Kloksie as soon as possible.

Customers may be asked to provide:

  • Order number
  • Photos of the product
  • Photos of the packaging
  • Photos of the shipping label
  • A description of the issue
  • Video evidence where reasonably necessary

Kloksie will review the issue and provide an appropriate remedy, which may include replacement, repair, refund, or another legally appropriate solution.

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive or restrict rights granted to consumers under applicable law.

18. Return Conditions

Where a return is approved, the product should generally be:

  • In its original condition
  • Unworn
  • Unwashed
  • Free from customer-caused damage
  • Returned with applicable tags, packaging, or accessories where reasonably required

Products that have been altered, damaged, washed, worn beyond reasonable inspection, or otherwise damaged by the customer may not qualify for a discretionary return or exchange.

This does not affect rights relating to defective or non-conforming goods under applicable law.

19. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, Kloksie will process the refund using the applicable payment method or another appropriate method agreed upon with the customer.

Processing time may depend on the payment provider, bank, e-wallet, or other financial institution. Kloksie cannot control delays caused by a third-party payment processor or financial institution.

Where applicable, shipping fees and other charges may be treated separately from the product purchase price, subject to applicable law.

20. Refund for Non-Conforming Products

If a product is confirmed to be defective, Kloksie will provide the appropriate remedy required under applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, the remedy may include replacement, repair, refund, or another appropriate resolution.

21. Store Credit

Where legally permitted and where the customer agrees, Kloksie may offer store credit instead of a monetary refund for certain discretionary returns, exchanges, or goodwill resolutions.

Store credit may be subject to terms communicated to the customer at the time it is issued.

Store credit will not be used to remove or restrict a customer's mandatory legal rights.

22. Non-Returnable / Refundable Items

Unless otherwise required by applicable law, certain products may not be eligible for discretionary return or exchange, including:

  • Pre-orders
  • Items clearly identified as final sale
  • Customized or personalized items
  • Items damaged through customer misuse
  • Items altered after purchase
  • Items that have been washed or substantially worn
  • Other products specifically identified as non-returnable before purchase

This does not exclude legally required remedies for defective or non-conforming goods.

23. How to Request a Return or Refund

Contact Instagram: @kloksie.co.

Please provide:

  • Full name
  • Order number
  • Contact number
  • Product name
  • Reason for the request
  • Photos or other evidence where applicable

24. Customer Responsibility for Order Information

Customers are responsible for checking the product, size, color, quantity, shipping address, contact number, payment information, and other order details before completing checkout.

Kloksie should be contacted immediately if an error is discovered.

All website content, including product photographs, product descriptions, graphics, logos, branding, text, layout, design, and videos, is part of the Kloksie website content.
